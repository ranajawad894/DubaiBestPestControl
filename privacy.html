Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Dubai Best Pest Control is committed to protecting your personal information and privacy in compliance with UAE data regulations and standard industry practices.

Last Updated: October 2026

1 Information We Collect

When you interact with Dubai Best Pest Control via our website, telephone, WhatsApp, or booking forms, we may collect personal data necessary to provide reliable pest management services. This includes:

  • Full name, contact phone numbers, and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Property address in Dubai or UAE (villa number, building name, area, emirate) for dispatching technicians.
  • Email address for sending invoices, quotations, and warranty confirmations.
  • Information regarding your specific pest infestation type, property scale, and preferred appointment slots.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for operational and customer service purposes, including:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and dispatching certified pest control technicians to your property.
  • Communicating service updates, pricing estimates, and follow-up reminders.
  • Issuing official payment receipts and maintaining service warranty records.
  • Improving our website performance, user experience, and customer support response times.

3 Data Protection & Security in Dubai

We implement rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Customer data is stored securely on protected servers and is accessible only by authorized administrative and operations personnel.

4 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar digital trackers to enhance browsing convenience, analyze site traffic, and remember your preferences. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect certain interactive elements of our instant quote calculator and booking features.

5 Sharing of Information with Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Your information is shared only with trusted field operators or payment processors strictly involved in fulfilling your pest control service request, or when required by Dubai regulatory authorities under UAE law.

6 Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or ask for the deletion of your records from our active database by contacting us directly.
