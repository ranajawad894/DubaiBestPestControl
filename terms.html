2 Scope of Pest Control Services

We provide professional residential, commercial, and industrial pest management treatments across Dubai, UAE, adhering to Dubai Municipality guidelines. Our services cover:

  • Bed bug inspection, heat treatments, and chemical eradication.
  • Cockroach, ant, and crawling insect elimination.
  • Rodent baiting, trapping, and exclusion sealing.
  • Mosquito and flying insect fogging/spraying.
  • Termite pre-construction and post-construction preventative barriers.

3 Customer Responsibilities & Preparation

Effective pest control requires cooperation from the property owner or occupant. Clients are required to:

  • Ensure clear and safe access to all treatment areas, including electrical rooms, utility spaces, and attics if applicable.
  • Cover or store all food items, cooking utensils, and children’s toys prior to chemical application as advised by our technicians.
  • Vacate the premises during specific treatments (such as fumigation or heavy ULV fogging) for the mandated safety duration (typically 2 to 4 hours).
  • Safely relocate domestic pets, fish tanks, and house plants during treatment.
